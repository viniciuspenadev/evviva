# Imagens do conceito

Método: ferramenta integrada imagegen, sem CLI ou API externa configurada pelo projeto. As imagens são ilustrações fotográficas, não documentam produtos, pessoas, obras ou lojas reais. A referência fornecida à geração foi `docs/conceito-aprovado.png`, usada somente como referência visual, sem reproduzir os elementos de interface.

## Arquivos

- `src/assets/inspiration/kitchen.png`: cozinha / hero, original 1672 × 941.
- `src/assets/inspiration/living.png`: living, original 1448 × 1086.
- `src/assets/inspiration/closet.png`: closet, original 1448 × 1086.

O Astro produz as variantes WebP responsivas durante o build. A referência original e os PNGs foram preservados.

## Prompts utilizados

### Cozinha

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset: one clean 16:9 architectural hero photograph for a luxury Brazilian custom furniture website. Supplied image is ONLY style reference. Do not include ANY typography, UI, border, watermark or logo. Show a contemporary Brazilian apartment open kitchen and living: dark walnut floor-to-ceiling cabinetry on LEFT third left calm and uncluttered for later white copy overlay, lit niche, honed pale travertine island in middle right, four walnut stools, cream linen sofa on far right, large window and sheer linen drapes, soft golden morning sunlight from right, distant leafy city view. Eye-level corrected verticals, exceptionally fine wood grain and stone texture, natural realistic professional editorial architectural photography. Entire output only photograph. No people, no letters. Match the architecture, warm walnut/ivory/olive palette and room quality in reference without any interface elements.

### Living

Use case: photorealistic-natural. One clean horizontal 4:3 interior photograph for a luxury furniture site, no text or UI. Reference only for materials and photographic style. A serene contemporary Brazilian living with sculptural low cream linen sofa, carefully crafted walnut wall panels and floating wood media console, round travertine coffee table, textured pale rug, one sculptural ceramic vase, floor to ceiling windows and linen curtains. Warm soft morning sunlight, tactile matte materials, restrained authentic luxury. A different individual room from the reference, same walnut/ivory/olive palette. Architectural eye level and corrected verticals, asymmetrical art direction, magazine photography. No people, logos, watermark, letters, website or collage.

### Closet

Use case: photorealistic-natural. One clean horizontal 4:3 architectural photograph of a luxury Brazilian walk-in closet. Supplied reference only for material and photographic quality, never include UI or typography. Dark walnut cabinetry and smoked glass with thin bronze structural details, warm integrated shelf lighting, precisely crafted drawers, shelves with neutral linen garments, low light taupe upholstered ottoman on pale honed stone floor. Architectural eye-level doorway view with strong depth, believable cabinetry, subtle window daylight. Contemporary restrained luxury, fine tactile wood grain and realistic joints, magazine interior photography. No people, text, logos, watermark or collage. Warm walnut/ivory/olive material palette matching reference.
